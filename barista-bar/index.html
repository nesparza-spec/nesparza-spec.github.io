Our Story

A bar for coffee lovers

The Barista Bar started with a simple idea: treat every cup like it matters. We source seasonal beans from ethical growers, roast in small batches, and pull every shot by hand.

Pull up a stool, chat with our baristas, and stay a while — the Wi-Fi is fast and the refills are friendly.
